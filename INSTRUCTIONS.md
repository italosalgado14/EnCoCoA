# Idea
The objective of this proyect is generate a detection aplication of voice of two different person to practice english, and write to a file the speaked conversation.

One microphone record the two voices and differentiate automatically what voice is from who. The conversation always be 2 persons. 

The second idea is make corrections of the detected speach, after the conversation is finished. The conversation are 10 minutes mean. Before these time, we analyce the text and make a suggestion of the sentences speaked. The objective is to improve our english, practicing with this application.

first of all make a plan to implement this. The idea is make an cheap and local way of do this. Using cloud services or APIs external is only allowed as last option. The idea is a lightweight and local program.
